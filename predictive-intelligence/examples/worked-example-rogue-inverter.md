# Worked Example: "The Rogue Inverter" (DSI)

## Step-by-Step Construction

This example shows how to build a predictive assessment from raw intelligence to finished product.

---

### Phase 1: Grounding Data Collection

**Raw intelligence gathered via web search:**
- May 2025: Rogue communication devices discovered in Chinese-manufactured solar inverters and batteries installed across the US
- The devices enabled unauthorized remote access that bypassed firewalls
- 70% of solar and battery components (including inverters and sensors) are manufactured in China
- 90% of global solar products contain at least one critical Chinese-made element
- UK renewables companies face approximately 1,000 attempted cyberattacks per day
- December 2025: Russia's Sandworm APT deployed DynoWiper against Polish renewable energy infrastructure

**Source verification:**
- Rogue devices: Just Security (law/policy publication — credible)
- Chinese manufacturing share: PV Magazine (industry trade publication — credible)
- UK attack frequency: ESET (cybersecurity vendor — credible, may have commercial bias)
- Sandworm/DynoWiper: ESET Research (primary technical source — highly credible)

**Selected grounding data**: Rogue communication modules in Chinese inverters (May 2025) — most specific, most alarming, most verifiable.

---

### Phase 2: Structural Analysis

**Frameworks applied:**

1. **Chokepoint Analysis**: China controls 70% of solar component manufacturing. This is a supply chain chokepoint equivalent to TSMC's position in semiconductors. Unlike chips (where alternatives are being built), no comparable Western solar manufacturing pipeline exists at scale.

2. **Deployment-Governance Gap**: Millions of inverters with network connectivity installed across US/EU grids with zero security inspection regime. The hardware was deployed to meet climate targets — security was not a procurement criterion. No government has proposed a mandatory hardware inspection program. The gap between deployment and governance is measured in years, possibly decades.

3. **Pre-Positioning Doctrine**: Hidden communication modules have no legitimate operational purpose. They can only serve one function: remote access and control by an unauthorized party. This matches the pre-positioning pattern seen in Volt Typhoon (cyber) and China's cable-cutting vessel (physical) — capabilities deployed in peacetime for wartime activation.

4. **Cascading Failure**: A coordinated disruption of grid-connected inverters could cause: power instability → hospital/water/communications failure → economic disruption → public safety crisis. The distributed nature of solar means no single point of failure — but also no single point of defense.

**Analytical synthesis**: The energy transition is expanding the grid's attack surface by orders of magnitude. The old model (centralized, hardened power plants) is being replaced by a distributed model (millions of edge devices with network connectivity). The new model is physically distributed but the supply chain is concentrated — in the same nation that represents the primary strategic competitor.

---

### Phase 3: Projection

**Time horizon selection**: 2035 — when renewable penetration will exceed 50% in most Western grids (based on IEA projections and current deployment rates).

**Projection**: By 2035, the renewable energy grid will be the most consequential unsecured attack surface in Western nations. The volume of Chinese-manufactured components with network connectivity will be too large to retroactively inspect, too embedded to replace, and too distributed to defend with perimeter-based security models.

**Countervailing force**: Governments could mandate hardware inspection regimes for imported energy components within the next 3-5 years. However, this is unlikely before a major incident because: (a) inspection regimes are expensive, (b) they would disrupt supply chains needed for climate commitments, (c) no political constituency currently demands them.

---

### Phase 4: Final Assessment

### 1. "The Rogue Inverter"
**Chinese-manufactured solar inverters with hidden communication modules are already inside America's grid. The attack surface of the energy transition is undefended.**

In May 2025, rogue communication devices were found in Chinese solar inverters and batteries installed across the US — unauthorized remote access bypassing firewalls. 70% of solar components are Chinese-manufactured. The transition from centralized fossil fuel plants to millions of distributed endpoints means the grid's attack surface has expanded by orders of magnitude, and the components come from the adversary. Project forward: by 2035, renewable penetration will exceed 50% in most Western grids. The cybersecurity model hasn't caught up to the physics.

- **Grounding data**: Rogue devices in Chinese inverters (May 2025), 70% Chinese solar component share
- **Sources**: Just Security, PV Magazine, Industrial Cyber
- **Brand**: DSI
- **Projection horizon**: 2035
- **Target outlets**: Defense One, The National Interest, Foreign Policy

---

## What Makes This Assessment Work

1. **The grounding is inarguable**: Rogue devices were found. This is not speculation.
2. **The structural analysis is multi-layered**: Four frameworks applied, each reinforcing the others
3. **The projection follows logically**: If the attack surface is expanding and no defense is being built, the vulnerability grows
4. **The countervailing force is named**: Hardware inspection regimes — and the reasons they're unlikely are structural, not political
5. **The title is a metaphor that carries the thesis**: "The Rogue Inverter" makes the invisible visible
6. **The one-sentence thesis stops scrolling**: "Hidden communication modules are already inside America's grid" — this is an intelligence finding, not an opinion
