# Predictive Intelligence — Brand Routing Guide

## Decision Tree: Which Brand Gets This Assessment?

```
Is the core subject a TECHNICAL vulnerability, exploit, or defense gap?
├── YES → DSI
│   Is there a geopolitical dimension that EXCEEDS the technical?
│   ├── YES → Consider GISI (with technical grounding)
│   └── NO → DSI confirmed
│
Is the core subject a POWER DYNAMIC, alliance shift, or chokepoint?
├── YES → GISI
│   Is the chokepoint technological (AI, cyber, space)?
│   ├── YES, and the tech is the story → DSI or ARIA
│   └── YES, but the power dynamic is the story → GISI confirmed
│
Is the core subject an AI SYSTEM, model, governance framework, or autonomous weapon?
├── YES → ARIA
│   Is the AI system being used as a WEAPON in a geopolitical context?
│   ├── YES, and the geopolitics is the story → GISI
│   └── YES, but the AI capability is the story → ARIA confirmed
│
Does it fit none of the above?
└── Cross-brand piece — route to whichever brand's audience would care most
```

## Brand Voice Cheat Sheet

### DSI — The Technical Authority
**Readers**: CISOs, security practitioners, infrastructure operators, defense analysts
**They care about**: What's vulnerable, how it's exploited, what to defend
**Opening move**: "Imagine waking up to discover..."
**Closing move**: "What organisations need to understand right now"
**Avoid**: Moralizing, ideological framing, vague policy prescriptions
**Prose style**: Flowing analysis, no bullet points, similes that make the abstract tangible

### GISI — The Contrarian Strategist
**Readers**: Foreign policy analysts, diplomats, defense planners, Global South strategists
**They care about**: Who holds power, how it shifts, what the West isn't seeing
**Opening move**: Provocative question or historical parallel that reframes the present
**Closing move**: A reframing statement that makes the reader see the headline differently
**Avoid**: Western-centric assumptions, "both sides" false equivalence, moral lecturing
**Prose style**: Bold, unapologetic, extended analogies that carry entire arguments

### ARIA — The Practitioner's Warning
**Readers**: AI governance leads, CTOs, risk officers, defense procurement, regulators
**They care about**: What AI risk looks like operationally, not philosophically
**Opening move**: A specific AI incident or deployment fact that reveals structural vulnerability
**Closing move**: The operational implication — what this means for the practitioner's Monday morning
**Avoid**: AI hype, existential risk without operational grounding, "AI ethics" without enforcement mechanisms
**Prose style**: Research-grounded, incident-driven, paradox framing (the solution creates the problem)

## Cross-Brand Pieces

Some assessments legitimately span brands. In these cases:

1. **Route to the primary brand** based on where the core analytical insight lives
2. **Note the cross-brand relevance** in the metadata
3. **Consider a companion piece** in the secondary brand with a different angle

**Example**: "The Malware That Asks for Help"
- Primary: ARIA (AI capability is the core insight)
- Secondary: DSI (operational cybersecurity implications)
- GISI angle possible: Russia's doctrinal shift to AI-augmented warfare

**Example**: "Two Internets, Three Civilizations"
- Primary: GISI (geopolitical fragmentation is the core insight)
- Secondary: DSI (infrastructure and cable routing implications)
- ARIA angle possible: AI model access as the new digital divide
